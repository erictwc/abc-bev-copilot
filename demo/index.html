Order-to-Shelf Copilot

An AI assistant for a trade development manager at ABC-BEV-CO, a fictional energy-drink company. It answers from the company's order, production, inventory and promo tables (by writing SQL) and from its policy documents (by search), and it cites what it used. Every record and document is synthetic.

New here? What to ask, and what is in the data

The data runs from November 2024 to October 2026. Treat October 31, 2026 as today. Click any question to put it in the box.

The company

  • 4 brands, 5 flavors each, in 12 oz and 16 oz cans: 40 SKUs.
    Lumaro: Cinder Berry, Quartz Lemon, Tidal Melon, Neon Pear, Prism Punch
    Quillbolt: Comet Cranberry, Thunder Guava, Static Mango, Glacier Plum, Spark Tangerine
    Skerrix: Flint Apple, Mist Lychee, Dusk Papaya, Arc Pineapple, Gale Passionfruit
    Torvane: Velvet Cherry, Solar Grape, Frost Kiwi, Copper Peach, Midnight Yuzu
  • SKU IDs look like TRV-YUZ-C16 (Torvane Midnight Yuzu, 16 oz). Names work too.
  • 12 distributors, e.g. Brindlecross Beverage, Calderwick Distribution, Fenwhistle Distributing, Penmoor Beverage, Quarrytown Distributing, Rookhaven Beverage.
  • Retail chains: Corvane Market, Corvane Express, Mirelle Mart, Pellweather Foods, Stonefinch Grocer, Stonefinch Fuel Stop.
  • 3 warehouses: WH-EAST, WH-CENTRAL, WH-WEST.

Tables it can query

  • Distributor purchase orders (POs): status, dates, quantities, shipments
  • Co-packer production runs, with constraint notes
  • Weekly warehouse inventory: on hand, allocated, in transit
  • Retail promotions and whether each is authorized
  • Distributor weekly depletions: sold, samples, spoiled, damaged

Documents it can search

  • Metric library (fill rate, on-time-in-full, cover weeks, depletions, lift)
  • Policies: PO changes, allocation, promo authorization, escalation ownership, returns, distributor onboarding
  • Q4 2026 receiving and holiday schedule
  • A product sheet for each of the 20 flavors (caffeine, calories, shelf life, case size)

What it will not do

  • Forecast, or answer anything the data does not hold; it should say it does not know
  • Change any record: its database access is read-only
Replay, not live. The local model runs on a small CPU-only mini PC on a home network that is never exposed to the internet, so this page shows its real recorded answers to the 40 evaluation questions, exactly as they came out, with the time each one took.

Live answers run on a monthly demo budget with per-visitor limits. Questions are logged, with your IP address hashed, to keep the demo working and see what people ask; please do not type personal information.
