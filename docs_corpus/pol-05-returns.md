# Returns policy

This policy says when a distributor can return product or get credit for it.

## When returns are accepted

ABC-BEV-CO accepts returns only for product that was spoiled from the start, or for
product damaged during shipping.

The distributor gets a credit. The Commercial VP approves every return. The distributor
must report it within 14 days of receipt.

## Product damaged or spoiled after receipt

Product damaged or spoiled after the distributor has received it is the distributor's
cost.

## Short-dated product

Two rules apply, in this order.

- If ABC-BEV-CO shipped product with under 90 days of shelf life left, the distributor
  gets a credit. This rule applies however long the distributor has held the product.
- Otherwise, if the distributor has had the product longer than 14 days to sell it, it
  is the distributor's problem.

## Discontinued SKUs

When a SKU is discontinued, the distributor can either send back the stock it has left
or get credit for it. ABC-BEV-CO prefers credit, with the distributor destroying the
product.

## Recalls and quality problems

The Commercial VP handles recalls and quality problems with the product itself. The
distributor may get a credit.
