# Escalation ownership

This policy says who owns each kind of supply problem, who acts first and who has the
final say.

## Roles

| Role | Team | Reports to |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio Manager | Commercial | Commercial VP |
| Sales Operations | Commercial | Commercial VP |
| Supply Planning | Product | Product VP |
| Production Manager | Product | Product VP |
| Finance | Finance | Finance VP |
| Marketing Manager | Commercial | Commercial VP |

The Commercial VP, the Product VP and the Finance VP all report to ownership.

## Alerts

Sales Operations receives the alert for most issues. It passes each one to the owner who
can fix it, as listed below.

## Who owns what

| Problem | Owner | Who else acts | Final say |
|---|---|---|---|
| A distributor is short on a SKU | The distributor acts first | The Portfolio Manager makes sure the distributor acts | The distributor |
| A co-packer run slips, is cut short or is cancelled | Production Manager | Supply Planning helps fix it; the Portfolio Manager is the voice to the distributors | Production Manager |
| Stock is in the wrong warehouse, or a shipping or receiving error | Supply Planning | Portfolio Manager | Supply Planning |
| A promo has no clear authorization | Marketing Manager | Portfolio Manager; Sales Operations | Marketing Manager |
| A promoted SKU does not have enough stock | Marketing Manager | Portfolio Manager; Sales Operations | Marketing Manager |
| Two sources of data disagree | The owners of each of the two sources investigate | | Finance decides which is right |

## Response times and escalation

- The owner responds the same day.
- An issue not resolved within 24 hours goes to the owner's VP.
- An issue the VP has not resolved within a further 24 hours goes to ownership.
- When the people involved report to different VPs, their two VPs settle it together.
