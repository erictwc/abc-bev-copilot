# Promo authorization procedure

This procedure says how a promo is put forward and authorized, and what happens when a
promo is not authorized or its authorization is not recorded.

## Who can put a promo forward

The Marketing Manager or Sales Operations submits a promo. A retailer can also ask for
one; the Marketing Manager or Sales Operations then submits it.

## Who authorizes

The Commercial VP authorizes every promo.

## What a promo needs before it is authorized

- A planned lift.
- A stock and cover check.
- A budget.
- The ROI of any previous programs with the same retailer or chain.

## Lead time

A promo must be authorized at least 90 days before it starts, so that the product can be
in stock.

## Level

A promo can be authorized for a chain (store banner), for a retailer (parent company), or
for both.

## Promos that are not authorized

A promo that is not authorized does not run. If a retailer runs it anyway, the retailer
is not compensated for any charges.

## Authorization not recorded

If a promo's authorization is not recorded, as neither authorized nor not authorized, it
must be recorded. The person who submitted the promo is responsible for getting it
recorded. Until then the promo is treated as not authorized: it does not run, and the
retailer is not compensated for any charges.

## Changes after authorization

Any change to an authorized promo, such as its dates, SKU or planned lift, must be
authorized again.
