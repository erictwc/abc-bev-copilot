# PO change policy

This policy says what a distributor can change on a purchase order (PO) at each status:
open, confirmed, shipped and received.

## Before the PO is confirmed (open)

Until ABC-BEV-CO confirms a PO, the distributor can change anything on it.

## After the PO is confirmed

Once a PO is confirmed, only the requested date can change. Quantity, SKU and every
other field are fixed, and there is no tolerance for small changes.

- The first requested-date change is free. Each change after that costs $200.
- Moving the requested date earlier or later needs Supply Planning's approval.
- A confirmed PO can be cancelled only with a 25% restocking fee.

## Cut-off

Each PO has a pickup date, which is filled in once a carrier has been assigned. No
changes are allowed within 48 hours of the pickup date.

## Shipped and received POs

A shipped or received PO is corrected only if there was a packing or shipping mistake,
or damage.

ABC-BEV-CO does not take returns.
