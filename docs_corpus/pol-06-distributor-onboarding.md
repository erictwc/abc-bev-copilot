# Distributor onboarding checklist

The steps a new distributor goes through before and after its first order, and who is
responsible for each.

## Checklist

| Step | Responsible |
|---|---|
| Propose the new distributor | Sales Operations, or ownership |
| Approve taking on the new distributor | Commercial VP |
| Check the paperwork: signed distribution agreement, credit check, tax and licensing documents, banking details | Finance VP |
| Set the territory and retail accounts | Commercial VP |
| Set the distributor up in ABC-BEV-CO's systems: the account, pricing, the SKUs it can order, and the warehouse that serves it | Portfolio Manager |

Insurance documents are not required.

## Territory and accounts

A new distributor's territory can overlap with an existing distributor's, as long as
their channels do not overlap. For example, in South Florida one distributor could serve
only cruise ships while another serves on-premise and off-premise accounts.

## First order

Every new distributor's first order is a standard first order, based on the size of its
account base.

## Depletion reporting

ABC-BEV-CO needs API or feed access to the distributor's depletion reports. A distributor
that cannot provide that must email its depletion report every Friday.

## Review period

There is no trial or review period.
