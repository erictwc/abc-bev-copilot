# Interview: POL-05 Returns policy

Eric's answers, word for word, given in a Claude Code session on 2026-11-03 (D27).
`docs_corpus/pol-05-returns.md` is drafted from these answers only. Earlier, in the POL-01
interview, Eric said "there are no returns".

## Round 1

**1. No returns, ever?** Are there truly no returns, or are the POL-01 corrections the only exception?
> returns only for spoiled product or if there was damage during shipping

**2. How a correction works.** Credit, replacement or pickup? Who handles it, and how long does the distributor have to report it?
> credit

**3. Short-dated or expired product.** Does ABC-BEV-CO do anything, or is it the distributor's problem?
> depends on how long the product has been in stock. if they have had over a certain amount of days to sell it then its not our problem. If we shipped them product that was on the short end of its shelf life then we will help.

**4. Discontinued SKUs.** Can a distributor send back or get credit for what's left?
> yes

**5. Damaged or spoiled at the distributor.** Is product damaged after receipt entirely the distributor's cost?
> yes

**6. Recalls or quality problems.** Who handles it, and what does the distributor get?
> Comm VP. They could get credit

**7. Header.** Owner, and effective date (2026-10-01 unless Eric says otherwise).
> Comm VP
