## Round 1

**1. Who starts it.** Who proposes a new distributor, and who approves taking them on?
> comm vp

**2. Paperwork and checks.** What must be in place before a new distributor can order (e.g. signed distribution agreement, credit check, tax and licensing documents, insurance, banking details)? Who checks each one?
> Finance vp

**3. Territory and accounts.** Defined territory or account list? Who sets it, and can it overlap with an existing distributor?
> comm vp

**4. Setup in our systems.** What has to be set up before the first PO (e.g. the account, pricing, orderable SKUs, the serving warehouse)? Who does it?
> PM

**5. First order.** Rules such as a minimum quantity, prepayment, or limits until they have a history?
> yes, we have standard first orders based on their account base

**6. Reporting.** What must they send from the start (e.g. depletion reports), and how often?
> we need API or feed access to depletion reports. If they can't do that they must email every friday

**7. Review period.** Is there a trial or review period, and who decides whether they stay?
> no

**8. Header.** Owner, and effective date (2026-10-01 unless Eric says otherwise).
> Comm VP
