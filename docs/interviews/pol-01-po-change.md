# Interview: POL-01 PO change policy

Eric's answers, word for word, given in a Claude Code session on 2026-11-03 (D27).
`docs_corpus/pol-01-po-change.md` is drafted from these answers only.

## Round 1

**1. Open POs.** Before confirmation, what can a distributor change, and does anyone have to approve it?
> as long as we haven't confirmed they can change anything on the PO

**2. After confirmation.** What can still change, and who has to approve each kind of change?
> requested date

**3. Cost of late changes.** Does a change or cancellation after confirmation cost the distributor anything?
> one free requested data change, after that its $200 to move the date

**4. Cut-off.** How close to the requested date can a change still be made?
> no changes within 48 hours of pickup

**5. Small changes.** Is there a tolerance that needs no approval?
> no.

**6. Moving dates earlier.** Can a distributor pull a requested date earlier, and who decides?
> yes. Supply Planning has to approve.

**7. Shipped or received POs.** Can those change, or does everything after shipping go through the returns policy (POL-05)?
> there are no returns

**8. Header.** Owner, and effective date (2026-10-01 unless Eric says otherwise).
> Product VP
