## Round 1

**1. Who requests it.** Marketing Manager, Portfolio Manager, Sales Operations, or the retailer?
> MM or SO, retailer could also ask for it

**2. Who signs off.** One person or several? Does it depend on the promo's size?
> Commercial VP

**3. What's needed to authorize.** For example a planned lift, a stock or cover check, a budget.
> all of that, plus ROI on any previous programs with the same retailer/chain

**4. Lead time.** How far ahead of the start date must a promo be authorized?
> 90 days (need to make sure product is in stock)

**5. Not authorized (n).** Can it still run? What happens if a retailer runs it anyway?
> no. they wouldn't be compensated for any charges

**6. Authorization not recorded** (flag blank). How is it treated, and who fixes the record?
> must get it recorded. its on the person who submitted it

**7. Changes after authorization.** Does a change to dates, SKU or lift need authorizing again?
> yes

**8. Level.** Per chain (store banner) or per retailer (parent company)?
> could be either and/or both.

**9. Header.** Owner, and effective date (2026-10-01 unless Eric says otherwise).
> Comm VP
